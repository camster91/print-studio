# Research: Toronto Market, Legal and Production (November 2026)

Deep-research report behind `business-plan.md`. Prices on Etsy are USD unless marked; multiply by ~1.35–1.40 for CAD. Not legal advice.

**Bottom line:** all four starting products have proven demand, but Etsy's 2025 original-design rule and the US shipping changes reshape the plan. Lead with self-designed products (signs, QR stands, lithophane frames), sell mostly in Canada and locally in Toronto, keep licensed designs (battery mounts) off Etsy. The goal needs ~3–5 sales a month, so demand and time are the limits, not the printer.

## A) Market validation (Etsy signals)

**Streamer name sign** — demand real, competition heavy.
- "Custom 3D Printed LED Name Sign | Gamer Tag, YouTube, Twitch | USB" $64.99, 351 reviews.
- "Gamer Tag Sign… Custom Light Up Sign" $47.90, 389 reviews.
- Generic 3D-printed "On Air" boxes $12–$28 (150–170 reviews). Acrylic/LED-flex "neon" signs have 1,000–3,600+ reviews.
- Plain "ON AIR" is a commodity; a personalised channel-name sign is where 3D printing wins. Price $45–$65 CAD lit, $29–$35 unlit.

**Battery mounts** — high volume, low price, crowded.
- M18 holders: $8.95 (210+ reviews), 6-pack locking $12.99 (630), 5-in-1 $19.99 (227), mount/hanger $14 (480), tool rack $14 (887).
- STLs sell for $2–$15. Sell bigger bundles locally: 6-pack $29–$35 CAD, garage wall kit (6 battery + 3 tool) $45–$55 CAD.

**QR counter stands (B2B)** — strongest B2B signal.
- "Custom Google Review QR Code Sign, 3D Printed Countertop Stand" $20.49–$24.99, 810+ reviews.
- QR tabletop $9.74–$12.99 (193). Business-card holder + QR $65 (232–250). NFC tap-to-review $38.99 (51).
- One seller: single $17, double $23, +$3 logo. Your $25 / 3 for $59 / $15 setup fits. Upsell NFC (+$15–$20 for a ~$1 sticker).

**Lithophane light box** — best-proven gift.
- Shadow box lithophane $29.04 (3,200 reviews). Cube $34.79 (1,200). Magnetic 5x7 USB-C $33.99 (605). Others $29.99–$45 (278–478). Premium "memory lightbox" $75.
- $45–$55 CAD is market middle. Memorial and pet photos are the emotional, higher-priced angle.

**Runner-ups:** Gridfinity bins sell for cents; money is in custom drawer-fit sets ($34) and tool-specific organisers (1.8k reviews at $9–$12). Nameplates crowded. Planters bulky to ship.
**Missing niches:** exact-model device stands/mounts, replacement parts and hobby jigs, board game inserts ($25–$90 but 8–20 h prints). **Avoid** flexi animals (prices collapsed ~£14 → £4.50).

## B) Competitors — what winners do

- Variation menus (sizes + logo add-on), bundles (6-pack, 4-photo cube), "send your measurements" custom sets.
- Photos: dimension diagrams (mm + inch), in-use shots, lit vs unlit, sizes side by side, text overlay on first photo.
- Words that sell: Personalized, Memorial gift, USB-C, Fits [model], Locking design, "Made in Toronto, ships from Canada".
- Your edge: Canadian shipping speed, local pickup, design help (turn a logo into a clean two-colour print).

## C) Canadian shipping

- Etsy gives Canadian sellers Canada Post Small Business Level 4 rates on labels. **Since Aug 25, 2025 Etsy no longer sells Canada Post labels for US-bound packages** (use Chit Chats or ShipStation).
- Etsy fees (CA): $0.20 USD listing, 6.5% transaction incl. shipping, 3% + $0.25 CAD processing (4% intl), 0.50% regulatory. ~$4.75 on a $42 order.
- Tracked Packet USA roughly $12.70–$18.77 by weight, plus fuel surcharge (~25.75% Nov 2–8, 2026). Check the live calculator.
- Canada Post small-business promo to Dec 31, 2026: up to 38% off domestic Expedited, 42% off US.
- $30 seasonal fee on oversize/unpackaged items Nov 2–Jan 10.
- **2026 holiday deadlines:** Expedited national Dec 14 (regional Dec 18, local Dec 22); Regular national Dec 10; US Tracked/Small Packet Dec 11.
- **Store cutoffs:** custom orders Dec 4, in-stock Dec 11, local pickup Dec 21.
- **US:** de minimis suspended for Canada since Aug 29, 2025 (indefinite from Jun 24, 2026). Postal shipments pay duty even if CUSMA-qualifying; couriers can still claim CUSMA. Canada Post requires a Declaration ID. → Sell Canada-only year one, or US only for $60+ items with duties priced in.

## E) Ontario business and legal basics

- Business name: free under your own legal name; otherwise $60 for 5 years (Ontario Business Registry). Doesn't protect the name.
- GST/HST: register after $30,000 taxable sales over four quarters, counting **all** your self-employed income together. If you're already registered, print sales are taxable.
- Report on T2125; keep receipts; printer = capital cost allowance.
- CCPSA: keep supplier/sales records 6 years; report incidents within 2 days, written report within 10. Don't market to children.
- LED/electrical: Ontario needs CSA/cUL/cETL approval (CE/UL-only doesn't count). Use certified USB LED modules or battery pucks; never wire mains.
- Trademarks: no brand logos or "official" colour schemes. Use "compatible with … Not affiliated with …".
- Insurance: home policy usually excludes business. Ask about a home-business endorsement; markets often want $2M liability.
- Etsy rule (June 10, 2025): computer-made items "must be produced based on a seller's original design"; the "templated design" wording was removed.

## F) STL licensing (2026)

- **MakerWorld:** Standard Digital File License forbids selling prints. CC BY/BY-SA/CC0 OK with credit. Commercial License Memberships since Feb 2025 (~$10–$19.90 USD/month examples).
- **Printables Clubs:** tiers $3–$100 USD/month with optional commercial license; creators can exclude models.
- **Cubee3D Hive:** $10/month first year then $15 (1,800+ designs, sell anywhere); lifetime ~$219–$299; HivePlus $29/month + 10% royalty.
- **Thangs / Patreon / Cults3D:** per-designer terms; read each.
- **Lithophane generators:** ItsLitho, Bambu MakerLab ("Make My Lithophane") and 3dp.rocks all allow commercial use of output. Watch MakerWorld frames under the Standard license.
- **Recommendation:** design your own signs, stands, nameplates and frames. For mounts/Gridfinity, design your own or buy one $10–$15/month membership and sell those on your own site and locally, not Etsy.

## G) Production on Bambu Lab + AMS

| Product | Print time | Filament | Material (~$0.03/g) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Streamer sign 2–3 colours | 2.5–4 h | 80–130 g + purge | $3–$5 + LED $5–$10 |
| Battery mount | 30–60 min | 25–50 g PETG | $1–$2 |
| QR stand 2-colour | 1–1.5 h | 30–50 g | $1–$1.50 |
| Lithophane panel 100×150 | 3–5 h | 30–50 g white | $1–$1.50 |
| Lithophane box/frame | 2–4 h | 60–100 g | $2–$3 |
| Gridfinity drawer set | 6–15 h | 200–500 g | $6–$15 |

Purge reduction, biggest wins first:
1. Colour-by-layer design (1–2 swaps per print).
2. Batch 4–8 per plate (one documented case: 104 g waste → 1.5 g).
3. Flush into infill/support (keep prime tower on). Not with white/clear lithophane filament.
4. Flush into objects (spare dark parts). Bambu Wiki: 70 g → under 1 g on the Bambu Cube, ~30% longer print.
5. Flush multiplier ~0.5–0.6, recalculate, test dark→light changes.
6. Prefer light base + dark letters (dark→white needs most purge).

Capacity: ~150–250 print hours/month = 40–60 signs/lithophanes. **Bottleneck is design, packing and messages (15–30 min per custom order).** Three $49 signs ≈ $90 profit.

## H) Custom build requests and the website

Quote form fields:
1. Name, email, phone (optional), city/postal code
2. Request type: Personalised sign / QR or business stand / Lithophane / Replacement part / Organiser / Something else
3. Description + use (indoor/outdoor, heat, load)
4. Upload: photos, logo (SVG/PNG/PDF), STL/3MF/STEP — multiple, ~25 MB each
5. Dimensions L×W×H with mm/inch toggle + "I'll send measurements"
6. Quantity (bulk note at 5+ and 10+)
7. Colours: swatches of stocked filament + brand hex field
8. Material: Auto (recommended) / PLA / PETG / TPU
9. Budget: <$25 / $25–50 / $50–100 / $100–250 / $250+
10. Deadline date + rush (+25%)
11. Design file? Yes / No, design it for me
12. Delivery: Toronto pickup / Canada shipping / US on request
13. Checkboxes: I own the photo/logo rights; custom items non-refundable once printing starts
14. Reference link

Quoting: (machine h × $3–$4) + (g × $0.05–$0.08) + extras + packaging; $25 minimum; design fee $0 / $25–$40 / $40–$60 h; deposit 50% (100% under $60); 1 proof + 1 revision, extra $10; 5–7 business days, rush 2–3 days +25%; reply within 24–48 h.

Platform: own site (core) with a form that handles uploads → email + sheet; pay by Stripe/Square invoice, Interac e-Transfer, cash at pickup. Shopify only if 10+ catalogue products. Etsy for discovery of original designs.

Pages: Home, Shop, Custom Orders (form + 4-step how it works), For Businesses (QR/NFC, nameplates, bulk, free sample), Gallery, FAQ, About ("Toronto maker & web designer"), Policies, Contact.

Trust: real photos only, reviews, Made in Toronto, clear prices, certified USB LED note, lithophane turn-on video, pickup area (not full address).

Photography: lightbox or window + white foam board, phone on tripod. Hero, scale, layer-detail, lit vs unlit, in-use. Square, consistent background.

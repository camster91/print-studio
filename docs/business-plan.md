## Production rules (low waste)

- Design for **colour-by-layer**: base in one colour, raised letters/QR in another = 1 swap per print.
- **Batch** 4–8 copies per plate. Flush multiplier ~0.5–0.6. Flush into infill/objects.
- Lithophanes, mounts and Gridfinity are **single colour** (no AMS needed).
- PETG/ASA for garages and cars; PLA = indoor only.

## Rules that matter

- **Etsy (since June 2025):** 3D-printed items must be your **original design**. A bought commercial license doesn't count. Design your own signs, stands, frames.
- **US shipping:** Etsy no longer sells Canada Post labels to the US; US postal shipments owe duty even on CUSMA goods. Sell Canada-only in year one.
- **LED items:** use only cUL/CSA/cETL-marked USB LED modules. Don't wire mains power.
- **Trademarks:** "compatible with Milwaukee M18 batteries. Not affiliated with Milwaukee Tool." No brand logos.
- **HST:** register once all your income (web + prints) passes $30,000 over four quarters.
- **CCPSA:** keep a 6-year order log. Don't sell anything for kids.

## Custom build requests

- Quote formula: (machine hours × $3–$4) + (grams × $0.05–$0.08) + extras + packaging. Minimum order $25.
- Design fee: $0 for text/logo swaps; $25–$40 simple custom; $40–$60/h real CAD.
- Deposit 50% (100% under $60). One proof + one revision included. 5–7 business days; rush +25%.
- Holiday cutoff: custom orders by **Dec 4**; in-stock ship by Dec 11; local pickup to Dec 21.

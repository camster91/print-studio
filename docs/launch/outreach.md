## Walk-in script

“Hi, I’m Cameron. I make small 3D printed signs here in Toronto. I’m testing QR stands that help customers find a review page, menu or booking link. Would the owner or manager be open to seeing a sample? No problem if it isn’t a fit.”

Only make a personalised sample after the business confirms the destination and approves using its name/logo. A normal review page is not always Google's direct review link. Scan the exact QR on two phones after printing. Do not send customers to a review form pre-filled with five stars or reward only positive reviews.

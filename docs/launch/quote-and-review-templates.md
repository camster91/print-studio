## Invoice procedure - no automatic charge or send

1. Confirm account is Layerline, CAD and customer's billing email. Calculate `max(25, hours × 4 + grams × 0.08 + parts + packaging + design)`; rush adds 25%. This is a quote floor, not the final margin check. Check parts, time, fees and shipping.
2. Confirm tax treatment with the bookkeeper. Create a Stripe invoice for the agreed deposit, with the request ID and full scope. Put “50% deposit” or “payment in full” on the line. A draft invoice is not a paid order.
3. Review the hosted invoice and obtain Cameron’s approval to send it. Record the invoice ID and deposit paid against the request. Never print just because an invoice was sent.
4. Send proof after the agreed deposit is paid. Receive written approval, then move new → quoted → approved → printing → done. Record the balance invoice and payment before dispatch. No production starts without approval.

## Review request - one week after confirmed delivery

Subject: How did your [product] turn out?

Hi [name],

I hope your [product] is working well. If you have a minute, I’d appreciate an honest review: [verified Google review link]. Good or bad, your feedback helps. If anything needs attention, reply here and I’ll help.

If you’re happy to share a photo, please send one. May Layerline use your review and photo on our website, with your first name and product name? It’s completely optional.

Thanks,
Cameron

Send only to a real customer, once, after delivery. No reward, review gating or pre-filled star rating. Replace the Google link only after GBP verification. Ask permission before publishing; record the exact review, rating if provided, source, consent and date. Keep `reviews.json` empty until then. A reply without permission is feedback, not permission to publish.
